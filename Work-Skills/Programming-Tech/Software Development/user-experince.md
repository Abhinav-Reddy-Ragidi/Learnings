1. Roles — who is this user, and what does this role actually need?
2. Reach — can they get to what they want in a few clicks?
3. Find — is the stuff used most front-and-center, not buried?
4. Land — does each role open onto the screen that matters to them?
5. Show-only-allowed — hide/disable what they can't do; never a button that just says "denied."
6. Feedback — does every action say loading / done / failed?
7. Helpful errors — when it fails, does it say why and what to do next?
8. Empty states — does a blank screen guide the next step, not look broken?
9. Fast frequent flows — is the most-used task quick, not just reachable?
10. Scale — can they find one item among thousands (search / filter / paginate)?
11. Their words — do labels match the user's vocabulary, everywhere?
12. Speed — do heavy screens load fast, or feel broken?
13. Handoffs — when work passes between roles, does everyone get notified and see status?
